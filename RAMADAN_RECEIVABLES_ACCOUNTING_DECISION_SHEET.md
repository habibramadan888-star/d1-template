## First-Pass Ramadan Decision Update

Ramadan Habib approved Q1-Q9 receivables/accounting rule direction for future
receivables authority and production preflight input only:

- Rent due should automatically create a receivable.
- Short pay should remain outstanding debt.
- Repayment should allocate oldest-due-first.
- Overpayment should close the receivable and leave the extra amount as a
  separate credit/manual-review item.
- Voided payments should restore outstanding because invalid payments should
  not reduce debt.
- Deposits should be excluded from rent income by default.
- Deposit refunds/deductions should be separate deposit ledger movements, and
  only explicit approved offset/adjustment can affect rent receivables.
- Due today / overdue should use Asia/Dubai business date.
- Dashboard due / overdue / arrears should eventually come from receivables,
  but only after production preflight, migration/backfill, rollback, and final
  human cutover approval.

These decisions are not production approval.

- `APPROVE`
